Follow theLight

Only what the cursor touches is revealed

Technique

A CSS mask that the cursor pushes

The "lit" copy sits perfectly over the dim copy, but a radial-mask-image only exposes a small circle around the pointer. Move it and the text appears through the darkness.

◎

Layer + glow

A soft 340px radial glow eases behind the core dot, tinting and trailing to give the light a physical presence rather than a hard edge.

✦

Cheap and crisp

Only two CSS custom properties (--sp-x / --sp-y) update per frame — no canvas, no DOM writes beyond transform & mask position.
