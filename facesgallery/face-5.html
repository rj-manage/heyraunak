Our story

Fourteen years of live fire.

Rohan Kapoor opened Tandoor Nights in 2011 with one clay oven, two cooks and a stubborn belief: Indian food deserves the same respect as any grand cuisine — precise technique, honest ingredients and no shortcuts.

Today the same recipes — and the same stubbornness — run three kitchens across London, Mumbai and New York. Our gravies still rest overnight, our breads are still slapped onto the tandoor wall by hand, and the fire is still lit fresh every single afternoon.

We cook for the neighbourhood first and the guidebooks second. That order has never changed.
