Why Lumen

One canvas, built for the whole mess of making.

Whiteboards died because ideas outgrew them. Lumen is infinite in every direction — including the ones you have not thought of yet.

Infinite canvas

Pan forever in any direction. Frames, notes and sketches coexist at scale — zoom out and the whole plan appears like a city at night.

Real-time together

Cursors glide beside yours while teammates draw, comment and re-arrange. Cursor avatars appear in under a second, anywhere on Earth.

Template library

Retrospectives, roadmaps, moodboards and wedding-planning grids — 200+ canvases your team can fork in one click and make their own.

Comments that land

Pin a thought to any pixel. Mention a teammate, resolve threads, and turn decisions into tasks without leaving the canvas once.

Works offline

Flight mode is not an emergency. Keep drawing on the train; every change syncs the instant you are back, with zero merge drama.

Restores itself

Version history keeps every cursor stroke for 30 days. A bad afternoon of rearranging is one “restore this morning” away.

We replaced three tools — a whiteboard app, a docs editor and a slide deck — with one Lumen board that the whole company actually opens every morning.
