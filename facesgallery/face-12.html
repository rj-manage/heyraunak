Spa & wellness

Rituals drawn from the headland

Four treatments, each built around something gathered here — pine, sea salt, cedar or the morning air itself. Open daily 8:00 to 20:00.

  • Submerged in a heated bath of pine-infused mineral water, then wrapped and left to rest while a therapist works out knots from the shoulders and neck with warm juniper oil.

  • A full-body exfoliation with coarse sea salt and coconut oil, followed by a rhythmic massage with heated shells. Ends with an ocean-facing foot soak on the cliff deck.

  • Warm cedar-smoked clay is pressed into tired muscles before a slow, deep-tissue sequence. Ideal after long walks or the drive down from the city.

  • Begin the day on the sunrise platform with guided pranayama and a short walking meditation along the cliff path. Complimentary for all guests on Tuesday and Saturday.
