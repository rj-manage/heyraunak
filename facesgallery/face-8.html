How it works

Three soft steps to a bigger buffer.

No spreadsheets, no guilt trips, no “you should save more” lectures. Pillow handles the boring arithmetic.

01

Name a dream

A trip, a laptop, a wedding, six months of rent. Give it a face and a deadline — Pillow turns it into a gentle number.

02

Set one rule

“Round up every purchase to the nearest ₹50”, “₹150 a day on payday weeks”. One rule. Pillow does the rest quietly in the background.

03
