About

Good branding is quiet confidence — design that says the right thing, then steps back.

I started in print, where every millimetre and margin had to earn its place. That discipline still runs through everything I make — only now the canvas is a logo, an app, a shelf, or a whole brand system.

I work with founders and in-house teams who care about craft: helping them find a visual voice that is distinctive enough to stand out and honest enough to live with for a decade. Each project begins with listening and ends with a system your team can actually run.
