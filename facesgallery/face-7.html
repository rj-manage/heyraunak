Why Prism

A wallet that behaves like software should.

Six quiet superpowers under the glass. Everything else is decoration — and even that has a purpose.

True self-custody

Keys are generated and held on your device with hardware-grade encryption. Prism never sees a seed phrase, and can never move funds without you.

Hardware sync

Pair a Ledger or Trezor in two taps and watch one combined portfolio across cold storage and hot wallets — no duplicate counting.

Gas optimization

Prism watches mempool pressure and suggests the cheapest moment to send — saving an average of 31% in network fees every month.

Swap engine

One tap routes your trade across fourteen liquidity sources and splits orders when it saves you money. Slippage warnings before you sign.

Analytics

Realized vs unrealized gains, cost basis and fee history — rendered into a clear weekly report you will actually read.

24/7 human support

Real people on every timezone, median reply under four minutes. We never ask for your keys — and neither should anyone else.

Security first, always

Built so the worst day never happens.

Non-custodial by design

If Prism the company vanished tomorrow, every wallet keeps working. Keys are yours — that is the whole point.

Encrypted before it leaves

Transactions are signed on-device and broadcast encrypted. Your data is hashed locally — nothing identifiable is ever uploaded.

Open source & audited

Every line reviewed by Halborn and four independent researchers. A public bug bounty has run since day one — untouched for 18 months.

Supported networks

One pane, every chain you care about.

Add and remove networks freely. Prism auto-detects assets across chains and groups them for you.
